Security and feature update. With a proxy on, the bunker now starts through it and never connects directly, even when the proxy setting cannot be read. A full bunker app list refuses new apps instead of evicting one. Co-signed Bitcoin spends list every output and the fee, and are approved only in the app. Changing the date no longer shortens PIN lockouts. Accounts whose key cannot be read are shown and can be set aside. Restoring a backup works again. New: agent approvals.
