Security update. A certificate authority whose keys were compromised is no longer trusted for relay connections. Switching or removing an account now disconnects the bunker apps paired under it, and its remembered approvals no longer carry over. Revoking a bunker app cuts it off at once, even while the bunker is off. Stored settings that cannot be read now keep their restrictions instead of resetting. Bunker apps will ask once more for actions you had set to remember.
